Step 1 -- plan:
a. List of parts:
   - Ears: Pointed structures at the top of the head.
   - Head: Rounded upper section containing eyes, nose, and whiskers.
   - Eyes: Circular features on the face.
   - Nose and Mouth: Small central features below the eyes.
   - Whiskers: Long horizontal lines extending from the cheeks.
   - Body: Large central mass representing a sitting posture.
   - Tail: Long, curved appendage extending from the rear side.
   - Paws: Bottom-most contact points on the ground.

b. Part locations:
   - Ears: rows 2-4, cols 16-20 (left) and 28-32 (right).
   - Head: rows 5-10, cols 14-34.

   - Nose/Mouth: row 8, col 24; row 9, cols 23-25.
   - Whiskers: rows 7-9, cols 6-13 and 35-42.

   - Tail: rows 16-22, cols 37-46.
