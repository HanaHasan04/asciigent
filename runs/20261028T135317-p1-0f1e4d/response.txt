a. The bear will consist of the following parts:
   - Ears: Two rounded shapes at the top of the head.
   - Head: A rounded square/oval shape containing the eyes and muzzle.
   - Eyes: Two circular shapes inside the head.
   - Muzzle and Nose: A central area on the face with a nose.
   - Body: A large, rounded torso indicating a sitting posture.
   - Front Paws: Two smaller oval shapes resting in the center of the body.
   - Hind Feet: Two flat, horizontal shapes at the base of the bear.

b. Extents of each part:
   - Ears: rows 1-3, cols 17-31
   - Head: rows 3-9, cols 12-36
   - Eyes: row 5, cols 19-29
   - Muzzle: rows 7-8, cols 22-26
   - Body: rows 10-23, cols 5-43
   - Front Paws: rows 14-18, cols 15-33
   - Hind Feet: rows 20-23, cols 7-41

c. Character usage:
   - Verticals: `|` for the sides of the head, paws, and torso.
   - Horizontals: `-` and `_` for the tops of the ears, head, muzzle, and bottom
